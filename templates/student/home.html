Exam Rules & Proctoring Protocols

Before entering the examination room, you must acknowledge and accept our AI proctoring standards:

  • Live Video & Audio Feed: A clear, front-facing webcam and operational microphone must stay active throughout.
  • Environment Isolation: Multiple persons, second screens, or external hardware are strictly logged as violations.
  • Tab & Focus Locking: Switching browser tabs, minimizing the window, or unauthorized hotkeys will flag your session.
  • Single Continuous Attempt: The examination must be finalized in a single continuous session.

Unresolved anomalies will terminate the attempt and transmit an audit report to your instructor.
