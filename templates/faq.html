Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DL Procted Examination and our neural integrity engine

1. What is DL Procted Examination?

DL Procted Examination is an enterprise-grade AI automated examination and proctoring platform. It monitors candidates during assessments in real-time, checking for dual individuals, tab divergence, vocal cues, and unauthorized materials.

2. How does the facial verification pipeline function?

Prior to test entry, the candidate captures a calibration portrait. A 68-point neural landmark model extracts a face embedding. During the test, live camera feeds are continuously matched against this verified token.

3. What if my connection fluctuates during the assessment?

Candidate progress and responses are continuously saved to the server. If momentary network drops occur, the proctoring engine queues telemetry buffers and synchronizes upon re-establishing connection.

4. What triggers an automated proctoring violation?

Violations include: leaving the camera frame, multiple faces detected, detecting mobile devices/books via YOLOv8, unauthorized tab/window switching, or prolonged acoustic chatter.

5. How is privacy and video data managed?

We believe in ethical, privacy-respecting proctoring. Only anomalous violation instances and forensic snapshots are retained for instructor audit, under encrypted institutional storage policies.
